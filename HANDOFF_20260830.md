  Source convention, flagged in code as a guess. It was wrong, and it fails
  SILENTLY - no compile error, you walk in and nothing happens. Fixed in
  batch16 and preflight, and `exitpoint` added to `twin_of`'s prefix list,
  without which both mirrored triggers would have pointed at the same
  destination.
- **Jump pads arc, they do not blink.** `trigger_catapult` is a
  "Bouncepad/Fan Trigger" with `launch_speed` (FGD default 1000, we use 800)
  and `target` -> `info_target_server_only`. One of SHIPPING.md's three
  first-compile questions, answered off a file.
- **Probe spellings.** `citadel_trigger_teleport` is the only teleporter
  candidate that exists; all three sinner candidates are absent.
  `SPELLING_PROBE` is off and the tables carry their verdicts.
- **Lane 0 is legal** on the proxy's sub-objectives, and the four slots are a
  FOUR-LANE ARTIFACT - the colour list still has Blue in it, and outputs are
  declared for slots 1 and 2 only.
- **Titan means PATRON.** `npc_boss_tier3` is tool-named "Patron" and
  described as "Tier3 Bosses/Titans/Patrons"; `destroyable_building` is
  "Base Shrine". The 2026-08-27 reading had the right entity and the wrong
  word. No code changed - the minimap already labelled them correctly.
- **Sinner is not the vault.** `ENeutralTrooperType` lists 6 "Sinner's
  Sacrifice" and 12 "Breakable Vault" separately. Sinners are now their own
  tier at type 6, keeping `neutral_camp_vaults` as a BORROWED subclass.
- **Camp spawn timings are inert** - both keys commented out and annotated

- **`building_health` and `final` on the shrine are marked "(Broken)".**
- **`Kill` is on base.fgd's `GameEntity` class**, so every entity answers it.
  That is why it appears in twelve fixture connections and nowhere in
  citadel.fgd.

## 2. Guardians are spawn markers, not NPCs

The lane guardian is `info_super_trooper_spawn`, tool-named "Lane Guardian",
carrying a `BossName` from a fixed list. It fires `OnTrooperKilled`, and it
is the only class in citadel.fgd that declares that output. All nine
guardian-closes-the-shop wires in the fixture are owned by it. The census has
17 of them and ZERO `npc_boss_tier1`.

We were placing `npc_boss_tier1` and wiring `OnBossKilled`. Both wrong, both
now fixed, and they had to change together.

**Why the old reading was reasonable**: `npc_units.vdata` really does carry
`npc_boss_tier1` with the brazier guardian model at 5500 health. But that
file lists UNITS, not map entity classes. The unit is what gets spawned; the
marker is what you place. Worth keeping in mind - the vdata will mislead the
same way again.

`BossName` is an ENUMERATION, `boss_<team>_t1_<colour>`, with the SINGULAR
"rebel". Our composed names matched nothing. batch13 and batch16 both needed
an `EXPLICIT_SWAPS` table because the generic rebels<->combine rule cannot
see "rebel" - the same trap twice.

## 3. The proxy is used, and it IS the win condition

citadel.fgd marks `citadel_final_objective_proxy` "Unused. Do not use." - the
only class labelled that way. On that reading it was dropped, and nothing
could replace it, because the FGD declares no input on `npc_boss_tier3` to
fire. The map briefly shipped with no shrine-to-patron chain at all.

Then the probe found dl_example wires it SIXTEEN times, and two of those are
`FinalShielded -> Trigger` and `FinalExposed -> Trigger` - the proxy's OWN
outputs. **The proxy computes the shielding from its sub-objectives and
announces it.** That is why the patron needs no input; nothing has to tell it
it is exposed. The chain was never meant to be hand-wired.

`EMIT_PROXY` is back on. Only slots 1 and 2 are used, and the fixture names
its sub-objectives left and right rather than by lane, which is the shape
`PROXY_SUBS = ["w", "e"]` already has.

## 4. The connection probe, and why it took three runs

`.github/workflows/connection-owner-probe.yml`. 89 of 89 attributed, no
disagreements. The three failures are worth reading before writing another
parser against this format:

1. **0 connections, exit green.** The fixture was being converted with
   `-oe keyvalues2_noids`, which strips element ids - so ownership was
   unrecoverable by any method. This one converts with `keyvalues2`. The
   parser then found nothing because element headers are QUOTED
   (`"DmeConnectionData"` inside an array, `"key" "TypeName"` under a key)
   and it expected bare type names. A quoted line is a header only if the
   next non-blank line opens a brace, so the fix was a lookahead.
2. **All 89 owned by `point_worldtext`.** Brackets are not braces: the
   parser popped the element stack on `]` as well as `}` but only pushed on
   `{`, so every array collapsed the tree.
3. Working.

Both failures now have guards: parsed-count vs mention-count, and a hard
error if more than ten connections resolve to one owner. Neither failure will
be printed as a result again.

**Unresolved targets are PREFAB-SCOPED.** Everything unresolved carries a
`125_` prefix or is a grate/ladder name; everything resolved has no prefix.
Those entities live inside prefabs, not in dl_example.vmap. That is why the
lid's brush class could not be read, and why `sub_objective_1` reads
`125_rebels_titan_yellow` while the shrines are named
`rebels_t3_generator_yellow`.

**It emits nothing, because `MIDBOSS_EVENT = ""`.** PROBE.md item 5's "the
midboss camp fires OnTrooperKilled" is WRONG: every midboss class in
citadel.fgd - `info_neutral_trooper_camp`, `info_mid_boss_spawn`,
`info_neutral_trooper_spawn`, `trigger_midboss_shield`,
`citadel_base_prop_midboss_indicator` - declares NO outputs and NO inputs at
all. Nothing on the map fires when the midboss dies. The only route is the
game event, and its name is in the vpk or the binaries, not in anything we
hold.

**The night is a SNAP by design**, timed to land with the global midboss

fallback rather than used. `post_processing_volume.fadetime` must be 0 if one
is ever added - its default is 1.0.

**`env_sky` has no inputs**, so the sky cannot change at runtime. Night comes
from fog, sun and post-processing under a fixed sky.
